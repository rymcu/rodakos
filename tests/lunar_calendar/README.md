# Lunar calendar

Host test for `main/phone_os/lunar_calendar.*`, the offline Chinese lunar calendar used by
Calendar. It needs no LVGL or ESP-IDF.

`reference.txt` and `main/phone_os/lunar_calendar_data.inc` are both generated by
`gen_lunar.py` from `lunar_python` (6tail, MIT), which follows the Purple Mountain Observatory
tables. The fixture lists the solar date every lunar month starts on (which pins every month
length and leap month) and the 24 solar term days for every year from 1900 to 2100.

The 5 cases check every month start and every solar term against the fixture, walk every day
of the range to confirm lunar days advance by one and months end on their stated length,
check known dates and Chinese names (2026-11-26 is 十月十八 丙午马年, 2027-02-05/06 are
除夕/春节, 2025 has 闰六月), and reject out-of-range or invalid dates.

```sh
cmake -S tests/lunar_calendar -B "$HOME/.cache/rodakos-lunar" -G Ninja -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Debug
cmake --build "$HOME/.cache/rodakos-lunar"
ctest --test-dir "$HOME/.cache/rodakos-lunar" --output-on-failure
```

To regenerate after a table change: `pip install lunar_python`, then
`python tests/lunar_calendar/gen_lunar.py .` from the repository root.
